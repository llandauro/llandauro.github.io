About me

I am a graduating student from McGill University with a B.Sc. in Computer Science. I am passionate about using robotics and computer vision to preserve marine environments and empower the Latin American community.

I first discovered robotics while interning at the McGill Mobile Robotics Lab, where I supported PhD candidate J.-F. Tremblay in his research on autonomous off-road traversability, published at the International Conference on Robotics and Automation.

Now part of the McGill Robotics Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Software team, I built the computer vision software architecture for underwater object detection. Our robot participated in the RoboSub 2025 Competition, where we won three different awards.

During McGill's study trip to Costa Rica, my team and I partnered with the NGO Sostenibilidad en Grande to optimize their data collection process. Our goal was to gain a better understanding of how microplastics affect the temperatures of turtle nesting beaches, as turtles' gender is temperature-dependent during incubation. We laid the groundwork for automatically classifying microplastic fragments in the sand using computer vision, paving the way for future applications.
