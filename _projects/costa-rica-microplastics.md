## Why it matters

The sex of a sea turtle depends on the temperature of the sand during incubation. Understanding how microplastics affect the temperature of nesting beaches starts with reliable data on how much plastic is there.

## Our work

During a 10-day field study with McGill's Desautels Faculty of Management, my team visited local partners to assess how microplastic data is collected.

- We **replaced paper records with structured digital tools**, removing manual re-entry into spreadsheets and saving 3 hours per microplastics tracing process.
- We researched **computer vision devices to count microplastics automatically** and contacted vendors about possible partnerships.
- We developed automated social media workflows and a content calendar to recruit volunteers.
