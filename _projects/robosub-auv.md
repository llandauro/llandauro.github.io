## The challenge

RoboSub is a competition for autonomous underwater vehicles: the robot completes a course on its own, finding and recognizing objects through moving, refracting water. I was selected to represent McGill Robotics at RoboSub 2025 in Irvine, California, against top university teams.

## My part

I built the computer vision system from end to end: connecting the cameras, collecting image data, **training a YOLOv8 model**, and enabling the object detection that the vehicle uses to navigate.

I worked with the electrical, mechanical and software teams, and contributed to the team's documentation.

The following season, I **guided the incoming students through the computer vision pipeline**, as they had to recreate it in ROS 2.

## Results

Our team won [**three awards at RoboSub 2025**](https://robosub.org/2025/08/18/robosub-2025-final-standings/), among 58 teams from nine countries:

- **Top Presentation**
- 3rd place in Design Documentation
- 3rd place in the MathWorks Simulation Challenge

I stayed on the team until December 2025. At RoboSub 2026, the team placed [**6th in the Autonomy Challenge**](https://robosub.org/2026/07/17/robosub-2026-final-standings/).
